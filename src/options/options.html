How to use on Tatkal day

  1. The day before: fill everything here, open irctc.co.in, press Field check in the panel on each page and fix any red field with Pick. Run a Dry run with General quota to watch it fill the passenger form (it stops before Continue).
  2. 09:50 — open the IRCTC tab, log in yourself, keep the tab visible (background tabs get throttled). Press Arm (live). The panel syncs the clock.
  3. The extension searches, opens your train, selects the class and waits for 10:00:00 (+offset) to click Book Now, then fills passengers → Continue.
  4. You: type captchas, enter the Aadhaar OTP, scan the UPI QR. If IRCTC logs you out at any point, the login form is refilled instantly — you type the captcha and press Enter, and the flow resumes where it was.
